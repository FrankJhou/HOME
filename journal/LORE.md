# LORE

## Character: Astro

### Appearance
Astro is a boy who always carries a sword in his hand and has a star on his head.

### Backstory
Astro comes from a village called **Star Wall**. The village was once a magical place, but one day the magic disappeared — and that made the whole village sad.

### Motivation
Astro wants to bring his village's magic back. To do this, he sets out on a dangerous quest to travel to the **Kingdom of Magic** and retrieve the **Gem of Magic** to save his village.
